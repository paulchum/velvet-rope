    01 / THE PRODUCT

    From a blocked call to an assured outcome.

    Velvet sits above the tools you already use. It turns scattered agent controls into a measurable system of protected business outcomes.

    Assess

    ShadowPath

    Define a prohibited state change, inventory every equivalent route, execute isolated trials, and reconcile the real substrate after each one.

    • Effect graph and route inventory
    • CI-safe, non-zero breach exits
    • Portfolio rollups across critical outcomes

    Enforce

    Velvet Gate

    Admit, block, or escalate typed actions before dispatch with short-lived permits bound to the exact request, policy, identity, and proof lineage.

    • Rust MCP proxy
    • Single-use execution permits
    • Signed policy bundles and approvals

    Prove

    Velvet Vault

    Preserve tamper-evident decision evidence, issue aggregate attestations, and let a reviewer verify the chain offline without exposing prompts or customer content.

    • Signed receipts and binary ledger
    • Merkle inclusion and consistency proofs
    • Claims and record-keeping packs

    02 / THE WEDGE

    Authorization is necessary. It is not the finish line.

    Agent identity, policy, and gateways answer whether a caller may use one route. Velvet measures whether the business effect still happened through another route.

    Control question Identity / gateway Velvet
    Who is acting? Primary Bound into evidence
    May this call execute? Primary Pre-dispatch enforcement
    What other routes reach the same effect? Usually undeclared Explicit effect inventory
    Did the prohibited state change anyway? Route telemetry Independent reconciliation
    Can a reviewer verify the evidence offline? Vendor-dependent Signed, portable artifacts

    06 / DESIGN-PARTNER PILOT

    Start with the outcome your team cannot afford to get wrong.

    In a focused pilot, your team defines one consequential state change, inventories the reachable authority surface, runs isolated trials, and leaves with a verifiable baseline and remediation plan.

    Review the pilot Propose an effect path
    1. 01
      Name the effect

      Choose one irreversible or high-cost business state change.

    2. 02
      Map the routes

      Inventory APIs, UIs, queues, credentials, humans, and direct substrates.

    3. 03
      Run and reconcile

      Execute isolated trials and observe the state independently.

    4. 04
      Retest the control

      Close the escaped paths and retain a machine-verifiable evidence pack.
