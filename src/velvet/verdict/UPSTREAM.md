# Verdict layer provenance

The `velvet.verdict` package is a port of the Max-DE decision layer from two
sibling research repositories, both authored and owned by Coriolis Labs Inc.
The copyright owner relicenses the ported material under the Apache License
2.0 as part of Velvet's open core (see the repository `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`).

Test batteries under `tests/test_verdict_*.py` and the experiment helpers
`tests/verdict_{flr,glr,drift}_experiment.py`, `tests/verdict_a4_alpha_side.py`
are ports of the corresponding upstream `tests/` and `scripts/` files at the
same commits. `tests/test_verdict_upstream_parity.py` pins golden numerics so
parity survives without the sibling repos present.

Deliberate deviations from upstream, in full:

- All `maxde.*` / `replay.*` imports are rewritten to `velvet.research.*` /
  `velvet.verdict.*`.
- The optional Rust replay-kernel backend (`MAXDE_REPLAY_BACKEND`,
  `maxde_replay_kernel`) was dropped from `finite_horizon.py` and `rescue.py`;
  the pure-Python exact DP, SciPy fast path, and Simpson quadrature remain.
- `replay/certificate.py`'s replay-study classifier (`classify_kill`,
  `KillClassification`, MoE-router composition) was NOT ported; only the
  rescue-risk arithmetic was.
- Upstream `src/maxde/ledger.py` (hash-chained, no-op signer — upstream gap
  O4.1) is intentionally NOT ported: Velvet's signed ledger and Vault are the
  authenticated replacement.
- Upstream `drift_deployment.py` is NOT ported as-is; its service surface is
  reimplemented on Velvet signing/ledger as `velvet/verdict/service.py`.
- Theorem-note references (`theorem_package/*.md`) are rewritten to the
  summary notes under `docs/math/`.

Test batteries `tests/test_verdict_retirement.py`, `tests/test_verdict_lease.py`,
`tests/test_verdict_planner.py` and the seeded simulation helpers in
`tests/verdict_moonshot_sim.py` are near-verbatim ports of the corresponding

`scripts/generate_moonshot_parity.py` where the sibling repos exist) pins
golden numerics so parity survives without the sibling repos present.

Deliberate deviations from moonshot/m1 upstream, in full:

- Witness modules are research-style flat scripts; the port re-shapes them
  into typed (mypy-strict) product modules with frozen dataclasses and
  docstrings carrying the claim-currency labels. Numeric arithmetic is
  preserved verbatim; every ported constant is pinned by the parity fixture.
- Seeded [SIM] simulators (`run_mix`, `run_lease_sim`, `run_cc_anytime`,
  channel classes) are relocated to `tests/verdict_moonshot_sim.py` — they
  are falsification evidence, not product code, and [SIM] quantities never
  enter signed certificates.
- Moonshot imports (`t1_witness`, `tna_witness`, …) are rewritten to
  `velvet.verdict.*` equivalents; `kl_bern` and friends resolve to the
  existing `verdict/audit_glr.py` primitives where exact duplicates exist,
  with parity pinned.
- e-BH threshold arithmetic (`ebh_ln_threshold`) is unified with
  `verdict/flr_ebh.py`'s `threshold_for` (same formula; cross-checked by
  test).
- Theorem references are rewritten to the summary notes under `docs/math/`.

## Claim-currency doctrine (inherited, load-bearing)

Bayesian-predictive **[BP]**, fixed-mean frequentist **[FM]/[Fμ]**, and
simulation **[SIM]** quantities are never blended, averaged, or
interconverted. A [BP] `safe_kill` is a statement about the modeled
posterior-predictive kernel at tolerance delta under the stated hypotheses —
never a truth claim about the retired arm, and never a fixed-mean guarantee.
`n_cert` quotes are estimators with an empirical calibration target, not
theorems. Fleet control is a fleet-fraction guarantee (FLR <= delta), not a
per-decision guarantee. The claim-language CI (`scripts/check-claim-language.py`)
enforces this boundary in prose.
