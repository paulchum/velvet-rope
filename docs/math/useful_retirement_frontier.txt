The Useful-Retirement Frontier
==============================

Claim currency: [FM] — every probability statement is fixed-mean frequentist, for an
adaptive one-arm-per-round Bernoulli bandit policy. No Bayesian-predictive martingale
is used, and no [BP] certificate implies this frontier.

Theorem 1 (post-lockout absorption).
------------------------------------
Let mu, mu' in (0,1)^K differ only in coordinate a, and run the same adaptive policy
under both. On every strict-lockout history with tau_a < infinity, for all
t >= tau_a,

    log dP_mu/dP_mu' |_{F_t}  =  log dP_mu/dP_mu' |_{F_{tau_a}}:

the locked arm's likelihood ratio FREEZES at retirement (policy-selection factors
cancel; non-candidate factors equal one; strict lockout supplies no candidate factor
after tau_a). Equivalently, conditional on a common observed history at retirement,
the law of the post-retirement observed continuation is identical under mu and mu'.
No monitor measurable from later selected actions and their realized rewards can
acquire further information about mu_a — later host data cannot learn the locked arm
back. Scope: pre-retirement histories may differ in probability, and other arms can
still reveal their own misspecification; the claim is only about worlds differing
solely in the locked arm. Strict lockout is needed only for this absorption
statement, not for Theorem 2.

Theorem 2 (usefulness-conditioned retirement lower bound).
----------------------------------------------------------
Let nu, nu' in (0,1)^K be two instances driven by the same adaptive policy, and
suppose 0 < delta < p <= 1 with P_nu(retire a) >= p and P_nu'(retire a) <= delta.
Then

    sum_{i=1}^K E_nu[ N_i(tau_a) ] * kl(nu_i, nu_i')  >=  d(p, delta),

where d is binary relative entropy. The usefulness condition p > delta is
load-bearing: a rule that never retires has P(retire) = 0, uses no samples, and is
VALID BUT USELESS — no positive sample lower bound can apply to it. Useful
retirement, not validity, is what carries a price.

Bernoulli frontier (the exact inspection price).
------------------------------------------------
Fix mu with arm a suboptimal; mu*^{-a} = max_{b != a} mu_b and
Delta_a = mu*^{-a} - mu_a > 0. If the rule is uniformly delta-safe for a (retirement
probability <= delta under every alternative raising only mu_a above mu*^{-a}) and it
retires a under mu with probability >= p > delta, then

    E_mu N_a(tau_a)  >=  d(p, delta) / kl(mu_a, mu*^{-a}),
    E_mu R_{tau_a}   >=  Delta_a * d(p, delta) / kl(mu_a, mu*^{-a}),

with R_{tau_a} the pre-retirement fixed-mean regret. As delta -> 0 at fixed p,
d(p, delta) = p log(1/delta) + O_p(1); at p -> 1 the leading retirement price is
log(1/delta) / kl — the constant a matching audit must attain. The directed Family-M
audit (anytime_glr_audit_family_m.txt, Theorem T3.2-DIR) supplies the matching
two-arm upper bound, so the two-arm directed frontier is exact.

Relation to DE and Max-DE. The unmodified greedy-host DE counterexample supplies a
positive-probability strict lockout of the truly optimal arm; Theorem 1 explains why
later host data cannot repair the missing information. Theorem 2 explains the repair:
a rule that usefully retires suboptimal arms while protecting optimal alternatives
must purchase enough pre-retirement inspections. The audit layer is what pays this
frontier from actual samples.

Hypotheses. Bernoulli rewards; the policy adaptive and measurable in the observed
history plus fresh policy randomization; tau_a an observed-filtration stopping time
(with the limit convention for never-retiring paths); interior means for the boxed
forms; the uniform delta-safety class as stated (alternatives change only the
candidate's coordinate).

Not claimed.
------------
- The change-of-measure inequality is classical; the contribution is its
  usefulness-conditioned formulation for irreversible retirement and its composition
  with the absorption theorem.
- No infinite-horizon Bayesian-predictive certificate, and no claim about artificial
  inspection clocks.
- A [BP] crossing certificate may license a model-relative lockout at tolerance
  delta, but it does NOT imply this fixed-mean frontier, and this frontier says
  nothing about the [BP] certificate's own validity.
- No claim that the frontier is attained beyond the predeclared two-arm directed
  case; a simulation or proof synopsis alone would not establish attainment.
