Adaptive Spend Safety with High-Probability Certificates
=======================================================

This note is separate from the deterministic hard-cap theorem in
docs/math/budget_safety_deterministic_theorem.txt.

It is also separate from the future swarm-layer reservation draft in
docs/math/swarm_layer_budget_safety_note.txt. That note describes aggregate sharded
reservation and bounded-stale local-pool projection above per-action
certificates. This note remains the per-action/adaptive spend envelope used by
the current runtime.

1. Boundary of the claims
-------------------------

The deterministic hard-cap certificate is pathwise. If the pre-execution ledger
spend is S_{t-1}, the certified hard cap is u_t, and

    S_{t-1} + u_t <= B,

then every execution path whose realized cost is at most u_t stays within the
budget B. This is the only deterministic no-overspend claim.

The adaptive spend certificates in this note are probabilistic. They certify
that, under the stated filtration, cost model, and moment/CGF assumptions, the
aggregate spend envelope is below B with probability at least 1 - delta. They do
not say that every path is below B. Outside the certified region, Velvet must
not claim budget safety.

2. Filtration and adaptedness
-----------------------------

Let (F_t) be the pre-execution filtration generated by the prior thread, prior
ledger records, and all observations used to issue the certificate. Admission
decisions, lambda choices, mixture weights, mean terms, and CGF or moment
bounds must be F_{t-1}-predictable. A certificate is stale if its filtration
hash, pre-ledger hash, ledger sequence, budget limit, or observed spend differs
from the runtime ledger snapshot.

Realized cost must be recorded after execution. If realized cost is missing, the
ledger fails closed for future probabilistic budget authority.

3. CGF/Ville certificate
------------------------

For predictable spend increments, suppose the certificate provides cumulative
mean term A_t and predictable CGF controls Psi_t(lambda_j) on a positive lambda
grid with mixture weights w_j. For total failure probability delta, define

    U_t = min_j {
        A_t + (Psi_t(lambda_j) + log(1 / (delta * w_j))) / lambda_j
    }.

By exponentiating the centered spend process at each lambda_j and subtracting
the predictable CGF compensator, each lambda_j gives a nonnegative supermartingale.
Ville's inequality bounds the probability that any one compensated process
crosses 1 / (delta * w_j). A union/mixture allocation over j gives total failure
probability at most delta. Therefore the CGF/Ville certificate may authorize
only when the high-probability envelope, including already observed spend,
clears the remaining budget.

4. Moment-only Cantelli fallback
--------------------------------

When only a mean upper bound m and variance or scale bound s are available, the
fallback certificate is moment-only. Cantelli's inequality gives the one-sided
bound

    m + sqrt((1 - delta) / delta) * s <= b.

This is weaker than the CGF/Ville certificate and must be labeled as
moment-only / Cantelli. It is probabilistic, not deterministic, and authorizes
only when the Cantelli bound clears the budget.

5. Runtime obligations
----------------------

An honest adaptive spend certificate must bind:

    certificate_kind in {cgf_ville, moment_cantelli}
    budget_limit
    delta_total
    observed_spend
    certified_mean_sum or mean_upper
    cgf_sum_by_lambda, lambda_grid, and mixture_weights for CGF/Ville
    variance_upper or second_moment_upper for Cantelli
    filtration_hash
    pre_ledger_hash and ledger_sequence_before
    cost_model_id
    theorem_refs including this note

An estimate-only cost model or usd_estimate is not a certificate and cannot
authorize a budget-safety claim.
