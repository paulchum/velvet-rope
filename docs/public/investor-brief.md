# Velvet: investor brief

**Coriolis Labs · Paul Chumbe**

Velvet tests whether agent controls prevent a prohibited business outcome, enforces supported
actions at the relevant boundary, and preserves evidence that can be reviewed outside the agent.

[Discuss a pre-seed conversation](https://shadowpath.coriolislabs.ca/contact/?intent=investor) ·
[Public evidence](claims-evidence.md) · [Implementation status](../../IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md)

## Product thesis

The long-term direction is a control plane for consequential agent actions: reusable business
constraints, integrations that enforce them, independent observations, and checks that customers
rerun after changes. That direction is separate from today's local, self-hosted implementation.

A permission decision on one tool call does not, by itself, establish that a business constraint
held across other routes or that the executed operation matched the approved one.

## What exists

ShadowPath effect projects and portfolio results; a Rust MCP proxy and typed pre-dispatch
controls; exact-bound permits; tamper-evident records and offline verification; and a bounded
PostgreSQL refund reference that commits authorization, budget, operation identity, and journal
evidence together. Consult [implementation status](../../IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md) for exact scope.

The public examples are synthetic local fixtures and recorded reference implementations.
They are not customer deployments, production-payment protection, a vendor failure rate,
or an independent audit. See the [claim-by-claim evidence register](claims-evidence.md).

## Initial commercial motion

Offer one assisted staging assessment, with an indicative starting price of **C$7,500**.
The customer owns the rerunnable project and gets a baseline, limitations, remediation plan,
and one agreed retest. [The pilot scope](pilot.md) describes prerequisites and exclusions.

The proposed expansion is maintained regression coverage, additional workflows, and supported
enforcement. Recurring packaging, adoption effort, and willingness to pay remain validation
questions. An offer is not booked revenue or a customer commitment.

## Reuse and differentiation hypothesis

The proposed advantage is not a temporary missing gateway feature. It is a reusable library
of business constraints, adapters, observers, and regression evidence. Each deployment should
make the next one faster and the customer's next release easier to evaluate. Measure that;
do not assume it from the number of interesting findings.

## Milestones for the next capital

1. Paid, bounded deployments with named customer owners and concrete acceptance criteria.
2. A second deployment of the same workflow class with substantial integration reuse.
3. Customer-operated reruns after changes, followed by a real ongoing-coverage purchase decision.
4. Better installation, failure handling, evidence quality, and operational ownership for those integrations.

These are milestones to establish, not traction already achieved.

## Risks to diligence

Customer urgency and budget are unvalidated here. Gateways can add overlapping controls.
Incomplete route inventories, poor observers, misconfigured credentials, and privileged writers
can invalidate an assurance claim. Bespoke integration work may fail to become a repeatable
product. Hosted operations and additional hardening require work beyond a successful local demo.
