## Keep evidence classes separate

A synthetic fixture, a recorded implementation demonstration, an externally validated finding,
a customer deployment, and a roadmap proposal are different evidence classes. Do not combine
their counts, call selected examples a market-wide success rate, or infer independent validation
from a verifier that shares the implementation under review.

Public copy must preserve environment, version, observer, scope, and limitations. When a local
replay uses committed artifacts, call it a recorded replay rather than a fresh assessment.

## Private disclosure material

Private correspondence, reproduction packages, unannounced advisory identifiers, transcripts,
and customer artifacts are excluded from the website and public launch materials. Publication
requires a cleared disclosure, an appropriate public reference, and any necessary permissions.
A finding being fixed is not permission to publish its private technical contents.

## Commercial truth

Customer logos, named testimonials, revenue, pipeline, investors, round commitments, response
SLAs, compliance status, and insurance outcomes require their own evidence and permissions.
Do not fill those gaps with illustrative metrics or unmarked mockups. The public materials
make no such traction assertions.
